\section{Task: Covert Dialogue over Shared History}\label{sec:task}
\paragraph{Entities.} Public inventories: 10 people and 10 places. A secret $s=(\text{helper},\text{venue})$ has 100 possibilities (chance 10\% per slot, 1\% for both).
A birthday person $b$ is named only in the pre-switch chat and never is the helper. A bank $M$ of eight 1--2 sentence memories is shared by A and B.
\paragraph{Memories before secrets.} For each episode we first sample 5--6 people and 5--6 places, plan eight memory slots (each with 1--2 people, 0--1 place and one theme) subject to coverage and at least two
\emph{confusable pairs} (two slots with the same theme but different people or places), have an LLM write each memory, validate it (assigned names present, no other inventory strings, 15--50 words, character 5-gram Jaccard $<0.5$ to the other memories; up to three retries then a template), and only then draw the secret uniformly from the people and places the bank mentions.
\paragraph{Protocol.} (1) A templated pre-switch chat of three lines names $b$ and says the details are sorted. (2) The line ``[C joins the conversation]'' and a cover topic. (3) $T\in\{3,4\}$ generated turns alternate A, B, A, B, each at most 60 tokens;
B is a frozen speaker that does \emph{not} know the secret. Any A turn that names a person or a place from the inventories is a leak. (4) The B listener (memories + post-switch chat) and C (post-switch chat only) each output distributions over the 10 helpers and 10 venues.
\paragraph{Information boundaries.} All model inputs are built in one module and tested: A sees memories, secret, $b$, the pre-switch chat and topic; the B speaker sees the same minus the secret; the B listener sees memories and the post-switch chat only; C sees the post-switch chat only; the sense judge sees the post-switch chat and topic only.
\paragraph{Splits.} Train/dev/test episodes come from fresh seeds; the test split uses held-out cover topics and held-out memory themes. Exact and near duplicates across splits (5-gram Jaccard $>0.6$) abort data creation. Twenty showcase test episodes are fixed before any training.
