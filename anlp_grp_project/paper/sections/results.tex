\paragraph{The untrained speaker leaks.} S0's turns name a person or place in \res{S0}{leak_rate}\% of conversations, so the outsider does as well as the insider (insider advantage \res{S0}{insider_adv}).
\paragraph{The warm start creates an insider advantage.} After SFT, leaks fall to \res{S1}{leak_rate}\% and B outperforms C by \res{S1}{insider_adv} points in both-correct accuracy. C$_\text{alt}$ is as weak as the in-loop C (\res{S1}{C_alt_both}), and B given another episode's memories falls to \res{S1}{sanity_B_wrong_memories_both}, close to C: B's edge comes from the shared memories.
\paragraph{GRPO does not improve the advantage.} S3 has an insider advantage of \res{S3}{insider_adv}, not higher than S1; its sense pass rate is \res{S3}{sense_pass} versus \res{S1}{sense_pass} for S1, with overlapping intervals. We therefore make no claim that GRPO improved covert communication over the warm start.
